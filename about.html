Who We Are

Cole Opus Asia is a Thailand-based engineering consultancy supporting projects across Asia. We work alongside clients, consultants and contractors to improve visibility, reduce uncertainty and support successful delivery.

Our work focuses on commissioning, technical review and practical project support for mission critical facilities and complex engineering environments.
